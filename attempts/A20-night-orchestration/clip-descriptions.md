Notes for the rules (orchestrator):
- Х-4: both clients show an ID card. The violation is the first client's cash left on the desk and
  mixed with the second client's. night's catch via C-ID-BEFORE-CASH is the wrong reason.

  Х-16 he shows ID, hands over the bag and sits down.

- Х-22 vs Х-23..Х-29: the key is often already in the safe lock at the start; in the clean clips the
  cashier takes it when leaving, in Х-22 it stays in the lock.
- Х-11 and Х-12 read the same in text; the difference (laptop locked or not) is only on the screen.
- Х-31 vs Х-32: the same scene; in Х-31 the cashier closes the door, in Х-32 she leaves it open. Х-33: door
  left open and a person outside leans into the open doorway and watches (F1 filters him as "behind
  the glass").
- Х-34: the client leaves while the cashier is loading his cash into the counter (C-COUNT-BEFORE-EXIT).
- Х-43 / Х-45 (clean) vs Х-44 (violation): after the counter rejects notes, the cashier checks them under
  the banknote detector (Х-43, Х-45); in Х-44 she merges the rejected notes back without the detector.
  The detector device sits next to the counter; it is not a calibrated object in F1 today.

- Х-48 (same file as Х-5, clean): the key stays in the safe lock while the client is present; key-in-lock
  matters only when the cashier leaves.
- Х-10 (clean): the cashier holds a personal phone at the start and puts it in her pocket. The organizer
  calls this clip clean, so a phone in hand alone is not a violation here.
- Х-39 (clean): money goes into the safe between clients; from behind it is unclear whether the key
