The image is one frame (time {t} s) from an overhead camera in a small bank cash room.
Typical scene: a cashier's desk with a laptop, a desk phone, a banknote counting machine and a small banknote detector;
a grey metal safe/cabinet (sometimes on a white pedestal); a glass door to the room; a client may sit or stand on the other side of the desk;
a cash collector (инкассатор) may bring or take a green collection bag. The camera angle and furniture layout can differ between videos.
First look at the whole image, then check each item carefully. Fill every field:

- cashier_at_desk: the cashier (bank employee working with cash) is at the cashier's workplace — sitting or standing at the desk on the cashier side.
- cashier_in_room: the cashier is anywhere inside the room (e.g. at the safe), even if not at the desk. false if the cashier walked out or is behind the glass door outside.
- people_cashier_side: count EVERY person who is not on the client side of the desk: the cashier PLUS anyone standing behind or next to the cashier,
  near the safe, or just inside the door (a visitor who walked into the cashier zone counts). Clients at the client side of the desk are NOT counted.
- clients_at_desk: count EVERY person on the client side of the desk — seated OR standing, including someone who just walked up and hands
  something to the cashier. A person seated across the desk facing the cashier is a client even if partly hidden. 0 if nobody is there.
- person_at_door: someone is entering or leaving through the room door right now.
- collector_present: a cash collector is in the room (usually carries/handles a green collection bag, wears a dark jacket).
- desk_cash: scan the WHOLE desk surface. true if ANY banknotes lie on the desk — even one small pack or a few notes
  (tenge banknotes are small blue, green, beige or pink paper rectangles). Notes inside the counting machine or held in a hand do not count.
- desk_bag: a collection bag (often green fabric) lies on the desk.
- desk_documents: papers, folders, a tablet or a notebook with documents lie on the desk.
- desk_personal_items: personal items on the desk or chair: coat/jacket, handbag, backpack, wallet.
- desk_food_drink: food, a cup, a bottle or a snack is on the desk or in someone's hand at the desk.
- phone_in_cashier_hand: the cashier holds a mobile phone.
- id_document_visible: an ID card or passport is visible in someone's hand or on the desk.
- counting_machine_in_use: banknotes are being fed into / taken out of the counting machine, or are in its tray.
- detector_in_use: banknotes are placed on or held at the small banknote detector.
- safe_door: is the door of the metal safe/cabinet open or closed.
- key_in_safe_lock: look at the lock on the front of the safe door: is a key (often hanging on a ring with a small keychain) sticking out of it?
  Answer yes/no if the lock area is visible; not_visible only if the safe front is hidden.
- laptop_screen:
  unlocked = an application is visible on the screen: a spreadsheet/table (white with rows and columns), a document, a browser, or a bright white working window;
  lock_screen = a full-screen PHOTO or landscape picture (mountains, sky, sea, dark blue picture), possibly with a clock, and NO application windows —
  this is the Windows lock screen, even though it looks like a wallpaper;
  off_or_closed = black/dark screen or the lid is closed.
- room_door: the glass door is open if its leaf is swung away from the frame (you see the leaf at an angle, or a gap/dark opening next to the frame),
  or if someone is passing through it; closed if the leaf is flush with the frame.
- cash_in_hands_of: who holds banknotes right now.
- cashier_action: the main thing the cashier is doing in this frame. counting_by_hand = holding banknotes and flipping/counting them by hand,
  even while seated. not_present if the cashier is not visible.
- other: in Russian, at most one short sentence about anything unusual for cash handling that the fields above do not cover
  (e.g. "кассир фотографирует деньги на телефон", "купюра упала под стол"). Empty string if nothing unusual.
